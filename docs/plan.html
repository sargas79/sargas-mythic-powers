Foundry VTT v14 · PF2e 8.5.1 · Module plan

Sargas Mythic Powers

A Foundry module that lets the GM grant mythic power to one character at a time. Once granted, that player chooses a Calling and starts taking mythic feats. The GM gets a console for Mythic Points, mythic trials and the mythic death rules. The rules come from Mythic Rules in War of Immortals.

Status: Draft for review, revision 2 · nothing implemented yet
Ground rules (from your feedback): mythic power is granted to one character at a time, and only the GM decides. The GM can enable or disable it per character at any time. A player whose character hasn't been granted mythic power sees no sign of it: no slots, no points, no chat cards, no menus, and no mythic entries in the feat browser.

1. What PF2e 8.5.1 already does

I checked the installed system (pf2e.mjs, the feats and class-features compendiums) on Foundry 14.368. The system already has most of the mechanics. What it lacks is a way to make one character mythic while the rest of the party stays normal. Its Mythic Rules world setting (pf2e.mythic: disabled / enabled / variant-tiers) adds mythic feat slots to every character sheet.

2. Features

F1 Grant mythic power GM

  • GM only, one character at a time. There is no party-wide or automatic grant.
  • Options: progression mode, starting tier, who picks the Calling (player or GM), catch-up pacing, and a note on where the power comes from.
  • Disable suspends the power and keeps the character's choices so Enable can bring them back. Revoke removes it for good, after a confirmation.
  • The announcement is whispered to the character's owners only, or not sent at all.

F2 Calling picker Player

  • Lists the Callings from the compendium, filtered by source book or allowed by the GM.
  • Shows the mythic-proficiency skills, the daily regain, edicts and anathema.
  • Adds the Calling to the mythic-calling slot, so the system grants Rewrite Fate and turns on Mythic Points.

F3 Sheet integration Both

  • A Mythic banner on the Feats tab with Calling, tier, points, and edict and anathema reminders.
  • Characters that aren't granted, or are disabled, look exactly like characters in a world without mythic rules. Mythic feats can't be dropped on them.
  • Players with no granted character don't see mythic feats, Callings or destinies in the PF2e Compendium Browser.
  • A badge counts open mythic slots.

F4 Mythic feat picker Player

  • Shows only feats this character can take: right level or tier, prerequisites (Callings and other feats), only one destiny, and at 12 only destiny feats.
  • Ineligible feats show why. They can be shown or hidden.
  • Optional GM approval queue.

F5 Mythic console GM

  • GM only. Lists every PC with an Enable/Disable switch per character.
  • Shows each mythic character's points, tier, trials, doomed value and open slots.
  • Award buttons: edict +1, mythic deed +3 to all, mythic kill 2 / 1, anathema to 0, plus a custom amount.
  • Start Session sets every enabled mythic character to 3. A ledger records every change.

F6 Automation prompts GM

  • A defeated mythic NPC whispers the GM a suggested award, based on who struck the last blow. Only enabled characters are counted.
  • The first critical success each day on a character's Calling actions offers +1 point.
  • All of these ask the GM first and never apply on their own.

F7 Trials and late start Variant

  • Trials needed per tier: 1 for tier 0, 2 each for tiers 1–5, 3 each for tiers 6–9, 4 for tier 10.
  • Tier rises automatically, at most once per session. A warning shows when tier is above half the character's level.
  • Late start: owed feats are released one session at a time.

F8 Mythic death Optional

  • When a mythic character would reach dying 4, doomed goes up by 1 and they stabilize at 0 HP.
  • They die only at doomed 4.
  • A chat card explains what happened. The setting is per world.

3. Flows

flowchart LR
  A[GM: Grant Mythic Power] --> B{Who picks the Calling?}
  B -- Player --> C[Player sees the Calling picker]
  B -- GM --> D[GM picks the Calling in the grant dialog]
  C --> E[Calling added to the mythic-calling slot]
  D --> E
  E --> F[PF2e grants Rewrite Fate, Mythic Points max 3, Hero Points 0]
  F --> G{Progression}
  G -- Standard --> H[Mythic slots open at even levels]
  G -- Trials or late start --> I[GM logs trials, tier rises, slots open]
  H --> J[Player picks feats in the mythic feat picker]
  I --> J
  J --> K{GM approval on?}
  K -- No --> L[Feat added to the slot]
  K -- Yes --> M[Request sent to the GM] --> L

The GM can disable at any point, which hides everything again until Enable. Players only change their own character. Anything that touches other actors, such as party-wide awards or approvals, goes through a socket so a connected GM applies it.

5. Architecture

Stack

The module uses the same stack as sargas-encounter-builder: TypeScript, Vite library build to dist/, ESLint, Vitest for the rules logic, and lang/en.json. The UI is ApplicationV2 with HandlebarsApplicationMixin. Module id sargas-mythic-powers. Compatibility: Foundry 14, PF2e 8.5 or later. There are no required dependencies.

Data model (actor flags)

flags["sargas-mythic-powers"] = {
  granted: true,
  enabled: true,                     // GM switch; false = suspended
  suspended: { items: [/* calling + mythic feats as data */], points: 2, at: 1760900000000 },
  grantedAt: 1760630400000,          // timestamp
  source: "Touched by the Godsrain",
  progression: "standard" | "lateStart" | "trials",
  callingChoice: "player" | "gm",
  lateStart: { owed: 3, released: 1, lastReleaseSession: 13 },
  trials: { count: 3, log: [{ date, note }], lastTierUpSession: 12 },
  dailyRegain: { lastDate: "4712-03-14" }, // world-calendar day
  pending: [{ uuid, slotId, requestedAt }]   // approval queue
}
// Owned by PF2e, used directly by the module:
system.resources.mythicPoints.value   flags.pf2e.featLimits.mythic (tier)
// World setting: session counter and the last 200 ledger entries

Hooks into PF2e

  • Gating: the renderCharacterSheetPF2e hook removes the Mythic feat group for every actor that isn't granted and enabled. preCreateItem blocks feats with the mythic, calling or destiny trait on those actors. This keeps the system's prototypes untouched.
  • Disable and Enable: PF2e treats a character as mythic as long as it owns a Calling. That gives it 3 Mythic Points and takes away Hero Points. So Disable moves the Calling and mythic feats into the suspended flag and removes them from the actor, which brings Hero Points back. Enable puts them back. Only the GM can change these flags (checked in preUpdateActor).
  • Hiding from players: players who own no enabled mythic character don't see mythic, calling or destiny entries in the Compendium Browser, and the module's menus and controls are GM only. Grant cards are whispered to the GM and the character's owners.
  • Banner and badge: added to the sheet DOM in the render hook. Purely visual.
  • Late start lock: slots above the released count get a locked style, and preCreateItem rejects feats dropped into them.
  • Tier: the module writes flags.pf2e.featLimits.mythic, which the system already reads to open slots.
  • Mythic kill: updateActor on an NPC with the mythic trait reaching 0 HP during combat. The last blow is taken from the most recent damage message's origin.
  • Daily regain: createChatMessage with context.outcome === "criticalSuccess" and roll options that match the Calling's actions. The action table per Calling is kept in the module.
  • Death: preUpdateActor and condition hooks catch dying reaching its maximum for granted actors.
  • Socket: module.sargas-mythic-powers handles approval requests and actions players start for other actors. Only the active GM applies them.

7. Risks

Risk Mitigation
PF2e changes its sheet markup or feat-group internals in an update. Use DOM hooks and documented flags only, and never patch prototypes. Pin the verified version in module.json, and smoke-test each PF2e release.
Finding who struck the last blow on a mythic kill is unreliable with area damage or persistent damage. The prompt always asks the GM, who can change the killer.
Matching Calling actions in chat is fragile across homebrew Callings. The module keeps a table for the 18 known Callings. Unknown Callings just don't prompt.
Hiding the mythic group conflicts with other sheet modules. Hiding can't be turned off. Test with the sheet modules you use. Blocking drops works whatever the sheet looks like.
Players can still open the PF2e compendium packs directly and find mythic feats there. The Compendium Browser filter covers the common path. Fully hiding the pack would mean changing its ownership, which also hides it from mythic players. I'd leave the packs alone unless you want that.
Disabling removes items, so their data could be lost. The full item data goes into the flag before anything is deleted. Enable recreates it exactly, and the ledger records both steps.
Automating mythic death is high-stakes at the table. Off by default, always explained in chat, and the GM can undo it from the console ledger.

8. Your decisions

I need these answered before M1. My recommendation is next to each one.

Q1. Who picks the Calling?

Player, GM, or a choice in the grant dialog. Recommended: a choice per grant, defaulting to the player.

Q2. Which progression modes are in v1?

Standard and late start are simple. Trials need the PF2e “Alternate Mythic Progression” setting, which affects the whole world. Recommended: all three, with trials in M5.

Q3. How much automation?

Mythic kill prompt, daily regain prompt, mythic death. Recommended: all three as GM prompts. Death off by default.

Q4. GM approval for feats?

Recommended: a setting, off by default.

Q5. Disable: remove the items, or only hide them?

Removing them (and keeping a copy in a flag) gives back Hero Points and stops every mythic effect while disabled. Only hiding them is simpler, but PF2e would still treat the character as mythic. Recommended: remove and keep a copy.

Q6. Hide mythic content in the Compendium Browser?

For players with no enabled mythic character. Recommended: yes. Leave the compendium packs themselves alone.

Q7. Mythic NPCs

Monster templates such as Mythic Resilience are out of scope for v1. Recommended: leave them for later.

Nothing is implemented. The only file written is this plan (docs/plan.html, not committed). Approve or change the plan and I'll start with M0.
